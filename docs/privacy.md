# Privacy policy

CarbonLayer is a browser extension that shows a Graphite-style inbox and pull request page on github.com. This page
describes what it does with your data. Last updated 12 October 2026.

## What the extension accesses

- **Your github.com session.** The extension runs only on github.com pages. To show and act on pull requests it reads
  github.com pages and the JSON routes GitHub's own pages use, and posts your reviews, comments, merges and settings
  changes to github.com, all with the sign-in you already have in your browser. It never asks for a token or
  password and never signs in on your behalf.
- **Your settings.** Your inbox sections, chosen organization and repositories, and display preferences are kept in
  Chrome's extension storage, which Chrome may sync across the devices signed in to your Chrome profile.
- **Graphite, only when you ask.** "Import from Graphite" reads the sections of your signed-in Graphite inbox from the
  app.graphite.com tab you have open, once, when you click it. Nothing is sent to Graphite.

## What the extension does not do

- It collects no analytics, telemetry or crash reports.
- It sends no data to the extension's author or to any server other than github.com (and app.graphite.com only for
  the import above, read-only).
- It stores nothing outside your browser profile.
- It contains no remote code; everything it runs ships in the extension package.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone. The "Buy me a coffee" link opens buymeacoffee.com in a new tab only if you click it.

## Your choices

Uninstalling the extension removes its storage. The "GitHub | CarbonLayer" switch in GitHub's header restores
GitHub's own pages at any time without uninstalling.
